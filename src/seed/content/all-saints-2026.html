On Sunday 1 November the chapel kept the Feast of All Saints with a Sung Mass. Afterwards, parishioners young and old came dressed as saints and, one by one, came forward to tell everyone about the saint they had chosen.

[photos 1]

The feast honours all the saints in heaven, the canonised and the countless unknown, and reminds us that every one of us is called to join them.

Then came the fun. In the garden behind the chapel, children and adults took turns, helmeted and blindfolded, at breaking open the piñata, and Father Keiser took his turn too.

[photos 2]

Thank you to everyone who dressed up, prepared a few words about their saint, and helped make the day such a joy.
