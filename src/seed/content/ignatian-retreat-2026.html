The Spiritual Exercises are a compilation of meditations, prayers and contemplative practices developed by St Ignatius of Loyola to help people deepen their relationship with God. They are a powerful means of conversion and spiritual growth, and Frs Esposito and Ginoux look forward to leading retreatants through these five days.

  • When: Monday 14 to Saturday 19 December 2026
  • Where: Padre Pio Retreat Centre, 25 Kort Street, Rietvalleirand, Pretoria
  • Who: open to all adults, men and women
  • Preached by: Fr Esposito and Fr Ginoux
  • Cost: R2,700 per person

How to register

Registration forms are at the back of the chapel, or speak to Fr Keiser for more information. Complete the form and return it to Fr Ginoux. Registration and payment must be complete by 30 November.

Payment may be made in cash, given to Fr Ginoux before 30 November, or by EFT, using the bank details on the registration form with your name and “retreat” as the reference. If you need to pay in instalments, or have any dietary requirements, please let Fr Ginoux know by 30 November.

Arrival and departure

Please have lunch before you arrive, and arrive in time for the opening conference at 14:00 on Monday 14 December. You may leave after lunch, at around 13:00, on Saturday 19 December.

What to bring

Bedsheets and towels are provided. Please bring:

  • your own toiletries
  • the Christian Warfare book (you may borrow or buy one at the start of the retreat)
  • a copy of the Scriptures, if you have one
  • a daily missal
  • a crucifix for your room
  • a pen and notebook for the conferences
  • an alarm clock (no smartphones)

Electronic devices may not be used during the retreat.

Questions

For any questions, please email Fr Ginoux at e.ginoux@fsspx.email, or speak to Fr Keiser after Mass.

May St Ignatius obtain for us a fruitful retreat. Ad majorem Dei gloriam.
