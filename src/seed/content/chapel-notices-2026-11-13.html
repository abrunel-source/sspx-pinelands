November is the month of the Holy Souls. Fr Otto Keiser is with us from Friday 13 to Monday 16 November, and again from Friday 20 to Monday 23 November. Our chapel is served by Our Lady of Sorrows Priory in Roodepoort, Johannesburg.

This weekend

  • Saturday 14 November: Confessions at 07:30, the Rosary in the park in front of the chapel at 08:00, followed by Holy Mass at 08:30. Pop-up market from 10:00 to 14:00, with good quality second-hand goods on sale.
  • Sunday 15 November: after the 09:00 Sung Mass there will be refreshments in the hall. All are welcome. The repository will be open, so come and buy your Catholic items before Fr Keiser continues with the catechism lessons.

Later events

  • Recital, Saturday 21 November, 18:00, at the Old Chapel Music Room. A wonderful recital with mezzo-soprano Astrid Bohm, accompanied by Jan Hugo on piano. Tickets R180 on Quicket. Bring family and friends to enjoy the beautiful music of Schubert, Strauss and Mahler.
