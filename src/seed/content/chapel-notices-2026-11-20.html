Later events

  • All-night Adoration, Saturday 28 November, 19:30, to Sunday 29 November, 08:45. Advent, the first day of the liturgical year, is nearly upon us. Let us prepare ourselves by spending time with Our Lord in Adoration. “Those who trust in Him shall not be confounded.” The Adoration sign-up schedule is at the back of the chapel, and there will be refreshments in the hall for those who need them.
  • Annual Christmas Market, Saturday 5 December, 09:30–17:00. We need donations and volunteer help, for the day itself and for setting up and taking down. Please speak to Chantal or Nirrie after Mass if you can help. Thank you!

There is still time to sign up: forms are at the back of the chapel, or speak to Fr Keiser for more information. Registration and payment close on 30 November.
