November is the month of the Holy Souls. Fr Otto Keiser is with us from Friday 6 to Monday 9 November, and again from Friday 13 to Monday 16 November. Our chapel is served by Our Lady of Sorrows Priory in Roodepoort, Johannesburg.

This weekend

  • First Friday, 6 November: Holy Mass at 18:45, followed by Benediction, Confessions and the Rosary. Confessions are also heard from 17:45.
  • First Saturday, 7 November (Our Lady on Saturday): Holy Mass at 08:30, followed by Benediction, Confessions and the Rosary. Confessions are also heard from 07:30.
  • Sunday 8 November: after the 09:00 Sung Mass there will be refreshments in the hall. All are welcome.
  • Cemetery visits, which began last Sunday, end on Sunday 8 November. You are encouraged to visit and pray for the departed (plenary indulgences).
  • The repository will be closed, but there will be a second-hand book sale in the hall: come and stock up on good Catholic literature.
